# Governance and Boundary

AI Cyber Policy Compliance Auditor is an independent research and teaching prototype for cybersecurity policy review, control mapping, gap analysis, evidence triage, and audit-support workflows.

## Intended use

Acceptable uses include:

- Teaching cyber policy analysis and compliance-review workflows.
- Experimenting with control mapping and evidence coverage review.
- Testing explainable compliance-assistance methods.
- Generating draft audit artifacts for internal review.
- Comparing governance-analysis methods in research settings.

## Non-intended use

This project is not intended for:

- Legal advice.
- Formal regulatory certification.
- Automatic compliance pass/fail decisions.
- Official audit sign-off.
- Replacing auditors, security teams, risk officers, or legal counsel.
- Filing compliance claims without expert review.

## Human oversight

All outputs must be reviewed by qualified human stakeholders. AI-generated mappings, gap summaries, and evidence indicators should be treated as review prompts, not final determinations.

## Evidence boundary

Evidence coverage in this repository represents audit support only. Evidence presence does not automatically mean control effectiveness, regulatory sufficiency, or audit readiness.

## Deployment caution

Any real-world use requires:

1. Organization-specific governance context.
2. Policy provenance and version control.
3. Framework interpretation review.
4. Legal and compliance oversight.
5. Audit-trail integrity.
6. Access-control safeguards.
7. Review of model limitations and uncertainty.
