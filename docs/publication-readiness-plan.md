# Publication Readiness Plan

This repository can be extended into a stronger academic paper by focusing on explainable cybersecurity policy alignment, evidence-readiness scoring, and responsible human-in-the-loop compliance review.

## Possible research questions

1. How well can AI-assisted mapping support cybersecurity audit preparation?
2. What policy gaps are easiest or hardest to detect automatically?
3. How can human reviewers verify, reject, or refine AI-generated compliance suggestions?
4. What traceability design best supports responsible audit automation?
5. How should uncertainty be communicated in compliance-support systems?

## Methodology extensions

- Add benchmark policy corpora.
- Compare rule-based, semantic, and ML-assisted mapping.
- Evaluate explanation usefulness with human reviewers.
- Measure false positives and false negatives in gap detection.
- Add inter-reviewer agreement studies.
- Track uncertainty and review status for every recommendation.

## Suggested paper structure

1. Introduction and motivation.
2. Related work in cyber GRC and AI-assisted audit support.
3. System architecture.
4. Synthetic policy and evidence corpus.
5. Control-mapping and gap-analysis methodology.
6. Evaluation protocol.
7. Governance, ethics, and limitation analysis.
8. Future work.

## Publication boundary

This system should be framed as decision support and research infrastructure. It should not be presented as a certified audit solution or a replacement for qualified compliance professionals.
